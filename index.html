Questions
before you join.

Something else? Write to support@bacaapp.com

When can I use it?

We add people from the waitlist to the test on Google Play in small groups. You'll get an email with a link to install Squarely. Everyone on the list also hears the day it opens to all.

Why a Gmail address?

Google Play's test works with Google accounts, so we need the one you use on your Android phone. We won't share it, and we only write about Squarely.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Squarely is Android first. Join the waitlist and you'll hear if an iPhone version is coming.

Do I need internet on site?

No. Measuring, drawing, 3D and exports all work with no signal.

Where are my projects kept?

On your phone. There's no account, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you share a drawing yourself.

What will it cost?

It's free while we test. Founding designers get a special price when paid plans start.
